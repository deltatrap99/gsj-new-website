What is Research?

'Research' refers to the process in which a student will identify a topic of their own choosing based on their personal interests and curiosity and will develop an authentic project to answer an original question in a given field.

The Research Process

1

Choose Topic

The first step is choosing a topic to study that you have a personal interest or curiosity about!

2

Develop Question

After reading current published literature, you will develop a specific research question to investigate.

3

Create Plan

Collaborate with your Coach and Mentor to create a solid research plan prior to starting your study.

4

Conduct Research

Conduct research following the methodology outlined in your plan. This can be done in-person or remotely.

5

Draw Conclusions

Analyze collected data to draw conclusions. These conclusions will be original findings in your field.

6

Present Findings

Write an academic paper and create visual presentations to share your findings with the world!
