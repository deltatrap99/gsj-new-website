About GSJ

Who We Are

Global Science Journey is an in-depth knowledge and skills educational ecosystem built with the mission of accompanying young learners on their journey of growth and future development.

Bringing together leading educators and researchers from Vietnam and internationally across Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Engineering, Technology, and Business, we develop meaningful, practice-oriented programs grounded in a shared belief that every student can thrive when guided and supported in a way that is personal to them.

We continuously expand our network of partner universities, research institutes, and educational organizations worldwide, opening doors for students to access world-class academic environments and global competitions.

Guided by the philosophy that "Education is not about making students the same, but about helping each student become the best version of themselves," we design personalized academic pathways and career guidance based on each student's individual strengths, passions, and abilities. Beyond delivering knowledge, we help students build confidence, develop real skills, and find the motivation to live by the ideal of "Become a better version of yourself, every day."

visibility

Vision

To become an internationally recognized organization in career education and skills development for students. We are committed to building a sustainable talent ecosystem by equipping young people with strong academic foundations and practical competencies. Through this, we empower students to develop holistically, integrate confidently into the global community, and take meaningful steps toward becoming responsible global citizens.

Mission

To empower 1 million students to become the best version of themselves-confident, resilient, and successful in pursuing their future careers.

Partnership

GSJ collaborates with globally ranked universities, international foundations, and specialist education partners — connecting our students with academic mentorship, world-class learning environments, and early career exposure.

University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia

A member of Australia's prestigious Group of Eight and consistently ranked among the world's top universities. UNSW is internationally recognised for engineering, business, and applied sciences.

University of Sydney (USYD), Australia

Australia's oldest university and a founding member of the Group of Eight, with a global reputation spanning medicine, law, engineering, and the humanities.

National University of Singapore (NUS)

Singapore's flagship university and consistently the highest-ranked institution in Asia. NUS is a global centre for research and innovation.

Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore

One of the world's leading young universities and a global powerhouse in engineering and technology, known for research impact in AI, materials science, and sustainability.

Hemispheres Foundation, Singapore

A Singapore-based foundation dedicated to youth development and international educational exchange, with programmes designed around leadership, global citizenship, and cross-cultural learning.

Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)

Vietnam's foremost technical university and the country's leading centre for engineering and scientific research, with a long-standing reputation for academic rigour.

The English Connection

A trusted English language centre equipping students with both language proficiency and the essential skills of a 21st-century global citizen, spanning English, debate, mathematics, science, drama, and public speaking.

Kevin Doan English Center

A well-regarded English language centre serving students across Central Vietnam, with more than 13 years of experience in English language teaching and debate instruction.

CN Vision

An academy delivering integrated English, skills, summer camp, and coaching programmes, guided by Former Principal of the High School for Gifted Students in Foreign Languages.
