Still wondering if this is right for you?

Embarking on a research journey is a big decision. Whether you're unsure about the time commitment, choosing a topic, or finding the right mentor, tell us what's on your mind. We're happy to guide you!

Common Concerns

"Will I have enough time?"

Most of our students balance research with a full high-school workload. Our mentors help you set a realistic schedule, typically requiring just 3-5 hours a week.

"I don't know what to research."

That's completely normal! The first phase of the program is dedicated entirely to exploring your interests and brainstorming a unique topic alongside your mentor.

"Is my English good enough?"

We welcome students from all backgrounds. Your mentor will support you not just with research, but also with academic writing and communication skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is GSJ's Science Research Program? expand_more

GSJ's Science Research Program guides high school students through an research project in their chosen field, from forming a research question to presenting findings at local and international competitions.

2. Who can apply to GSJ's programs? expand_more

Our programs are open to students in grades 8 to 12 who are curious, motivated, and eager to explore academic research, business, or study abroad pathways.

3. What fields of research does GSJ support? expand_more

Students can pursue research across Natural Sciences, Technology and Engineering, Social Sciences, and Business and Finance,...

4. How long does a research project typically take? expand_more

Most research projects run over a period of 5 to 6 months, with regular sessions each week guided by a dedicated mentor and teaching assistant.

5. Will my child work with a real researcher or scientist? expand_more

Yes. Student is paired with a qualified mentor from an academic institution or professional background relevant to their research field.

6. Does GSJ offer study abroad consulting? expand_more

Yes. GSJ provides personalized study abroad consulting, supporting students with profile building, essay writing, university selection, and application preparation.

7. What competitions can students participate in through GSJ? expand_more

- Prestigious International Academic Competitions in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Social Sciences, and related disciplines (IBO, BBO, JCOO, UKChO, etc.)
- National and International Science and Engineering Competitions
- Innovation, STEM, and Technology Competitions: SVIIF, IIENA, iCAN, AI-JAM US, INTARG, INOVA Croatia, Innoverse, iWISE
- National and International Academic Competitions Across Specialized Disciplines

8. Does GSJ offer summer camps or international programs? expand_more

Yes. GSJ organizes international career exploration camps and summer programs in destinations such as Singapore and Australia, America,... designed to broaden students' academic horizons and expand their global network.

9. How does GSJ personalize its programs for each student? expand_more

GSJ designs individualized academic roadmaps based on each student's strengths, interests, and goals, ensuring guidance that is relevant and meaningful to their personal journey.

10. How can I get started with GSJ? expand_more

You can reach out to our team via our website or contact us directly to schedule a one-on-one consultation. Our staff will help identify the most suitable program for your child.
