We are here to listen

Still wondering if this is right for you?

Embarking on a research journey is a big decision. Whether you're unsure about the time commitment, choosing a topic, or finding the right mentor, tell us what's on your mind. We're happy to guide you!

Common Concerns

"Will I have enough time?"

Most of our students balance research with a full high-school workload. Our mentors help you set a realistic schedule, typically requiring just 3-5 hours a week.

"I don't know what to research."

That's completely normal! The first phase of the program is dedicated entirely to exploring your interests and brainstorming a unique topic alongside your mentor.

"Is my English good enough?"

We welcome students from all backgrounds. Your mentor will support you not just with research, but also with academic writing and communication skills.
