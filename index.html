Global Science Journey is an in-depth knowledge and skills educational ecosystem built with the mission of accompanying young learners on their journey of growth and future development.

Bringing together leading educators and researchers from Vietnam and internationally across Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Engineering, Technology, and Business, we develop meaningful, practice-oriented programs grounded in a shared belief that every student can thrive when guided and supported in a way that is personal to them.

We continuously expand our network of partner universities, research institutes, and educational organizations worldwide, opening doors for students to access world-class academic environments and global competitions.

Guided by the philosophy that "Education is not about making students the same, but about helping each student become the best version of themselves," we design personalized academic pathways and career guidance based on each student's individual strengths, passions, and abilities. Beyond delivering knowledge, we help students build confidence, develop real skills, and find the motivation to live by the ideal of "Become a better version of yourself, every day."

Choose Your Pathway ✦

On-Demand Programs

We present curated projects hand-selected by leading field experts and fully developed for immediate launch upon selection of a qualified student, offering an exclusive pathway to advanced, mentor-guided academic inquiry.

These projects are assigned using rolling admissions.

Summer Cohort

We offer a selective summer program designed for motivated students. The extended availability of the summer months allows participants to engage in deeper, more rigorous projects under the mentorship of experienced professionals. Candidates are expected to demonstrate strong academic preparation; only the most promising applicants will be admitted.

Application period: March 1–May 1 annually.
Selected participants begin projects in June or July.

Premium

Personalized Experience

Students selecting this premium pathway partner one-on-one with a GSJ mentor to craft an original, high-impact project from the ground up. Ideal for driven students who have already identified a compelling goal, this option guarantees personalized project design, targeted mentor matching with a vetted professional in the field, and guided launch support. Enrollment is limited to maintain bespoke attention.
