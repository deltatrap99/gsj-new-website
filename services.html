PROGRAM 02 / 09

biotech

Advanced Scientific Research

A research project built around your curiosity - with expert mentorship to take it further.

Disciplines: Business & Economics · Technology & Engineering · Natural Sciences · Social Sciences

The GSJ Research Program gives students full, structured access to the research process - the way it actually works in academia. Students are guided through every stage: literature review, research design, data collection and analysis, expert panel presentation, and the development of scientific manuscripts.

Research takes place inside real university and institute laboratories such as: VNU University of Science, Hanoi University of Pharmacy, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and the Institute of Biotechnology which giving students direct access to professional research environments, not simulations.

Designed for students pursuing serious academic growth, the program helps students develop:
✓ In-depth knowledge of specialized science, engineering, and technology fields.
✓ Mentorship from local and international experts; benefit from one-on-one career guidance with leading specialists.
✓ Develop teamwork, critical thinking, scientific poster design, presentation, and expert Q&A skills.
✓ Build meaningful academic and professional connections with experts worldwide.
✓ Research outputs for publications, conference presentations, and participation in national and international science and technology competitions, strengthening university application portfolios.

More than a research project — a foundation for your future career.

Coaching & Mentoring on Career Roadmap

Self-Understanding - Career Exploration - University & Scholarship Roadmap

A career orientation pathway of three specialized programs that helps students build the foundation for long-term academic and career decisions - from understanding themselves and exploring their abilities to experiencing real professional environments, choosing a suitable field, and pursuing their dream university with continuous mentorship.

Program 1: Self-Understanding - Motivation - Goal Setting
