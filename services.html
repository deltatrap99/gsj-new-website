Integrated STEM & Scientific Research

A comprehensive STEM foundation integrating advanced theory with intensive practical applications. We ignite a passion for science early on through hands-on experimental projects in modern, internationally-standardized laboratories.

Students are guided to conduct independent scientific research projects, culminating in participation in prestigious international competitions (such as ISEF and World Invention). Under the mentorship of leading scientists and field experts, students develop 21st-century skills: critical thinking, creativity, and advanced problem-solving. This pathway provides the rigor, structure, and depth of a premier research experience.

Advanced Business & Career Orientation

The Advanced Business School (ABS) equips the next generation with essential management, entrepreneurship, and leadership skills. This pathway is an intensive, immersive opportunity for students ready to pursue in-depth business education and practical career orientation.

Students participate in practical internships at major domestic and international corporations. Furthermore, our Global Youth Conferences connect students with a worldwide network of young leaders through academic forums. Outdoor team-building experiences and specialized school-based programs are also provided to help students discover and position their personal strengths early on.

Global Scholarship Strategy (Premium)

Our most comprehensive and elite package. GSJ doesn't just educate; we construct breakthrough portfolios to help you conquer the highest peaks of global education in the USA (Ivy League, Top 50), UK (Russell Group), and Australia (Go8).

Be paired with a professional strategist who will mentor you through every stage of the application. We customize a 70-100% scholarship strategy based on your unique strengths. By seamlessly integrating the results from your Scientific Research, ABS projects, and international standardized tests (SAT/IELTS), we create an undeniably powerful and unique application profile. This package is perfect for students who want a competitive edge and high-level support.

What's included with a membership?

When you join a GSJ program, you gain access to an exclusive educational ecosystem:

  • Professional Mentorship: You will be paired with a scientist or industry expert working in their field to collaborate on authentic projects.
  • GSJ Facilities & Portal: Access to our modern labs and our exclusive portal to manage tasks, milestones, and communicate with your mentors.
  • Research & Strategy Coaches: Access to our team of professionals with decades of experience working with high school students.
  • Resource Library & Priority Help: Templates, examples, and dedicated support staff to answer any questions you may have.

What does the application process look like?

Once you submit an application, it will be reviewed by the staff at GSJ. If your application meets our criteria, it will be passed along to the specific program director for review. We will then schedule virtual interviews with top candidates. Final selection occurs after the interview. If chosen, you will receive an acceptance letter and have a limited time to submit your enrollment to secure your spot.

What payment methods do you accept? Can I pay in installments?

We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers. Payments can be made either in full or spread out over monthly installments depending on the specific pathway you choose.
