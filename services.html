Scientific Research Program

Build a research project that is entirely yours

Disciplines: Business & Economics · Technology & Engineering · Natural Sciences · Social Sciences

The GSJ Scientific Research Program gives students full, structured access to the scientific research process - the way it actually works in academia. Students are guided through every stage: literature review, research design, data collection and analysis, expert panel presentation, and the development of publication-ready scientific manuscripts in both Vietnamese and English.

Research takes place inside real university and institute laboratories including VNU University of Science, Hanoi University of Pharmacy, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, and the Institute of Biotechnology which giving students direct access to professional research environments, not simulations.

Designed for students pursuing serious academic growth, the program helps students develop:

  • ✓ In-depth knowledge of specialized science, engineering, and technology fields.
  • ✓ Mentorship from local and international experts; benefit from one-on-one career guidance with leading specialists.
  • ✓ Develop teamwork, critical thinking, scientific poster design, presentation, and expert Q&A skills.
  • ✓ Build meaningful academic and professional connections with experts worldwide.
  • ✓ Research outputs for publications, conference presentations, and participation in national and international science and technology competitions, strengthening university application portfolios.

More than a research project - a foundation for future academic work.

What's included with a membership?

When you join a GSJ program, you gain access to an exclusive educational ecosystem:

  • Professional Mentorship: You will be paired with a scientist or industry expert working in their field to collaborate on authentic projects.
  • GSJ Facilities & Portal: Access to our modern labs and our exclusive portal to manage tasks, milestones, and communicate with your mentors.
  • Research & Strategy Coaches: Access to our team of professionals with decades of experience working with high school students.
  • Resource Library & Priority Help: Templates, examples, and dedicated support staff to answer any questions you may have.

What does the application process look like?

Once you submit an application, it will be reviewed by the staff at GSJ. If your application meets our criteria, it will be passed along to the specific program director for review. We will then schedule virtual interviews with top candidates. Final selection occurs after the interview. If chosen, you will receive an acceptance letter and have a limited time to submit your enrollment to secure your spot.

What payment methods do you accept? Can I pay in installments?

We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers. Payments can be made either in full or spread out over monthly installments depending on the specific pathway you choose.
