Who are our Research Coaches and Mentors?

At GSJ, we take pride in our diverse team of experts who come from various academic institutions and fields from around the world. Our seasoned coaches have years of experience teaching and advising. Our mentors have backgrounds in academia and the private sector, bringing a wealth of knowledge and experience to guide students in their endeavors. Whether you prefer in-person interactions or virtual collaborations, our mentors are available to support you every step of the way. Get ready to embark on a journey of discovery with our exceptional coaches and mentors at GSJ!
